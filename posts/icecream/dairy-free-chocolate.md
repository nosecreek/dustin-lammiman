## Dairy-Free Chocolate Ice Cream

### Ingredients

- 1/3 Cup Granulated Sugar
- 1/4 Cup Cocoa Powder
- 1/4 Tsp Salt
- 1/4 Tsp Xanthan Gum (optional)
- 1/3 Cup Water
- 1 Can Rooster Coconut Cream (400ml can, 18g fat per 83ml)
- 2 Tbsp Light Corn Syrup
- 1/4 Tsp Vanilla Extract

Xanthan gum is optional because Rooster brand Coconut Milk already contains guar gum, which already acts as a stabilizer for the ice cream.

### Instructions

1. In a large bowl whisk together sugar, cocoa powder, salt (and optional xanthan gum).
2. In a medium pot: mix water, coconut cream, and corn syrup.
3. Whisk dry ingredients into the pot of liquid ingredients. Be sure to mix well.
4. Heat ingredients for ~5 minutes on medium heat, until sugar is dissolved
5. Remove from heat and mix in vanilla extract.
6. Place mixture in a sealed contained and refrigerate for 12+ hours.
7. Churn according to your ice cream maker's instructions.
